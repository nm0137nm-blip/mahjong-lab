# CP2.5.9: automatic evidence learning
Purpose: complement the language coach with data-derived, interpretable tactical associations, not replace Mortal or claim to decode its internal reasons.
Pilot on the existing two generated matches (371 labeled decisions). Restrict to decisions whose entire legal set is discards: 232 included, 139 excluded. Fit a pairwise logistic ranker of Mortal's chosen action against worse legal discards using 25 visible features (shanten by form, ukeire, retained dora, honor/terminal/red, neighboring tiles, own copies, yakuhai, visible counts, riichi-specific genbutsu, and context interactions). Fixed C=0.1. Each training decision receives equal total weight. Fit scaling only on training decisions. Test on the other match, repeat in reverse. No LLM needed for this step.
Results: efficiency-led held out (147 cases): baseline 71 exact, normalized Q gap .129867; fitted 53 exact, gap .176714. S42-led held out (85 cases): baseline 49 exact, gap .154816; fitted 45 exact, gap .138107. Total 120/232 baseline vs 98/232 fitted. Baseline min shanten, max ukeire, max retained dora, then action index. Reject fitted proxy: no production change. Features are associations, not causal reasons. Only two matches, prior development exposed the corpus; not a certification set.
Next: collect 12 generated half-games (3 disjoint cohorts with four each, different explicit seeds, alternating S42-led and efficiency-led). Keep train, validation, holdout separate by whole match. Do not tune against holdout; once viewed, label consumed and replace with fresh cohort. No automatic conclusion based on the 2 reused contrast fixtures. No assumption that these matches represent expert human play. Do not use real user data or paid LLM API. The public repository's standard runner workflow is bounded, no scheduled or unbounded compute. 
Future automated training: evaluate models against fixed simple baseline, compare normalized Q gap and top-1 separately by tactical slice, use match-level uncertainty, assess candidate explanations and counterfactuals independently. Only export verified associations with sample counts, uncertainty, applicability and exceptions; human-readable coach rules may be selected by retrieval, not accumulated without limit. Human/LLM explanation quality still needs separate evaluation. ChatGPT Plus is not an unlimited programmable evaluation API; without external LLM or paid API, bulk LLM judgments are not currently automatic.
